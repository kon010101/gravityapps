Overview

EchoDex is a family-friendly Pokémon sound companion. Say a name, hear the cry, learn the moves.

  • Hear official-style cries for hundreds of Pokémon
  • Search by voice — say a name into the mic
  • Browse the full list and tap to explore
  • Listen to move / attack names out loud
  • Switch between English and German

How to use

  1. Tap the mic and say a Pokémon name
  2. Or type / pick from the grid
  3. Play the cry and hear move names
